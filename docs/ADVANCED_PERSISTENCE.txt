==========================================================
SENTINEL-Q: ADVANCED PERSISTENCE MANUAL
==========================================================

Design summary:
- `services` stores the current snapshot state.
- `incident_history` stores historical incident events.
- `log_incident()` records a row automatically on non-200 results.

Why two tables:
- the current state remains lightweight and query-friendly
- historical failures remain available for audit and reporting

Validation idea:
- trigger the same failing service multiple times
- confirm multiple rows appear in `incident_history`
