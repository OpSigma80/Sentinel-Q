SENTINEL-Q: ALERTING BUILD NOTES - PHASE 5
==========================================================

Alerting flow:
- the Checker detects a failing status
- the Notifier formats an outbound message
- `logger.critical()` marks high-severity events in logs

Design rationale:
- the Checker detects failures
- the Notifier communicates them
- this keeps the design extensible for future channels such as Telegram, SMS, or email
